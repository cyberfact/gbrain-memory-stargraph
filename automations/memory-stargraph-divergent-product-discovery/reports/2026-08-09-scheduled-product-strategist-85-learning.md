# Loaded Search Is A Proof Surface

After weekly outcomes, readiness, and terminal lifecycle receipts become product surfaces, loaded search itself becomes part of the proof contract.

Evidence from `runs/memory-stargraph-divergent-product-discovery-20260809t040110-0700-85`: health was loaded at V1.0.187, weekly verified memory outcomes passed 7/7, customer readiness was mostly ready, raw SG evidence pages were readable, active tags were clear, and resolver pending proposals were zero. Yet `/api/search` returned ok=true with zero results for exact IDs and known terms including SG-0162, SG-0186, SG-0188, SG-0191, SG-0192, `memory stargraph`, and `Ask Yoda`.

Reusable heuristic: if proof surfaces exist but loaded search cannot rediscover them, product strategy should prioritize search discoverability or truthful search-degraded feedback before adding more evidence cards. Users experience proof as trustworthy only when it is findable.
